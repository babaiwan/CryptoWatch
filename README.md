CryptoWatch is a lightweight cryptocurrency ticker plugin for IntelliJ IDEA. It lets you monitor real-time prices of Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major coins directly inside your IDE, without switching to a browser or opening a separate app. The plugin supports a stealth mode that blends price data into your editor theme, making it easy to keep an eye on the market while you code. Data is fetched from public exchange APIs and refreshed automatically at a configurable interval. You can customize your watchlist, choose quote currencies, and toggle between compact and detailed views. CryptoWatch is designed to be unobtrusive, fast, and privacy-friendly — your watchlist stays local.











CryptoWatch 是一款轻量级的 IntelliJ IDEA 加密货币看盘插件。你可以在 IDE 内直接查看比特币、以太坊等主流币种的实时价格，无需切换浏览器或打开独立应用。插件支持隐蔽模式，能将行情数据融入编辑器主题，方便你在写代码时随时关注市场。数据来自公开交易所 API，按可配置的间隔自动刷新。你可以自定义自选列表、选择计价货币，并在紧凑视图和详细视图之间切换。CryptoWatch 注重无干扰、快速和隐私友好——你的自选数据仅保存在本地。
